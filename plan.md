Good. We’re going pure product design thinking, not implementation.

You’re building a Personal Operating System Website — not a planner.

Below is a structured brainstorm of what this could become.

🧠 Core Identity

This isn’t just:

a task manager

a calendar

a homework tracker

It’s a decision engine for your life.

The goal:

Reduce friction + reduce overwhelm + increase intentionality.

🔹 1. The Command Center (Home Screen)
What it shows:

Current time

“You have 3h 12m free today”

Energy suggestion (“High-focus window active”)

Top 3 priorities

Next hard deadline countdown

Streak tracker (deep work days, tennis practice, etc.)

It should feel like:

“Here is what matters right now.”

🔹 2. Academic Brain
Assignment Hub

All homework in one place

Difficulty rating

Estimated time

% complete progress bars

“Likely to procrastinate” warning

Overdue risk indicator

Grade Risk Monitor

Predict grade if you skip X assignment

Show “this is worth 15% of unit grade”

Show GPA impact simulation

Study Optimizer

Auto-break assignments into chunks

Detect “you always underestimate math by 40%”

Track how long things actually take

🔹 3. Schedule Intelligence
Free Time Detector

Shows:

Total free time today

Longest uninterrupted block

Mental energy curve (morning high, evening low)

Time Debt Meter

If you skip something, it shows accumulated backlog pressure

Visual “overwhelm bar”

Adaptive Reshuffle

If you miss something:

It reassigns intelligently

Doesn’t just push everything later

🔹 4. Goal Engine (This is powerful)

Instead of just tasks:

You create:

“Become 5.5 UTR in tennis”

“1600 SAT”

“CMU-level CS skill”

“Finish LangLearner V1”

Each goal has:

Required weekly hours

Skill components

Progress velocity

Projected completion date

You can see:

If I train 3 hrs/week, I hit this by June.
If 5 hrs/week, by April.

🔹 5. Performance Analytics

You would LOVE this.

Weekly Review Page

Hours spent by category

% of planned vs actual

Focus score

Deep work streak

Tennis training minutes

Coding minutes

Heat Map

Like GitHub contributions — but for productivity.

🔹 6. Focus Mode

Minimal interface:

One task

Timer

Block distractions

Shows:

“Why this matters”

Goal it supports

Time remaining

After session:

“How hard was that?”

“Did you get distracted?”

Over time:

It learns your friction patterns.

Quick daily input:

Energy level

Stress level

Sleep hours

Workout done?

Then it correlates:

Low sleep → lower focus

Tennis practice improves next-day focus

Sports days reduce homework completion

It becomes self-quantified data.

3 years

Today

🔹 9. Decision Simulator

This is advanced but powerful.

“What happens if I skip this?”

GPA impact

Stress impact

Time debt

Goal delay

“What if I study 30 extra minutes per day?”

SAT projection shift

CS mastery timeline acceleration

🔹 10. Identity-Based System

Instead of:
“I have to do homework.”

It frames:
“You are becoming someone who…”

Examples:

Becoming a disciplined engineer

Becoming a D1-level athlete

Becoming top 1% in SAT

The dashboard reinforces identity daily.

School

Tennis

CS / Projects

Social

Health

Personal

⚠ “Skill stagnation risk.”

🔹 12. Burnout Prevention

Track:

Consecutive high-load days

Sleep trend

Overdue count

Then:

“High burnout probability next 5 days.”

🔹 13. Procrastination Predictor

Based on:

Task difficulty

Time of day

“You are 63% likely to delay this.”
